✦ Our story

Skincare shouldn't feel like guesswork.

Lumira started with a simple frustration: a bathroom shelf full of products that didn't work together, bought one confusing recommendation at a time.

Lumira was founded by Mara, who — like most people — learned skincare the expensive way: trial, error, and a drawer of half-used bottles that promised the world and delivered irritation. Not because the products were bad, but because nothing was actually chosen for her.

So Lumira starts with a diagnostic instead of a shelf. A few honest questions about your skin, and you get a routine built around what you actually need — morning and evening, with a plain reason for every step. No dermatology degree required, no jargon, no guessing.

We keep it deliberately simple: no medical questionnaires, no subscriptions you didn't ask for. Just a routine that makes sense, and a catalog of Lumira's own formulas behind it — so the answer to "does this actually work together" is always yes.

What we believe

Clarity over jargon

You shouldn't need to know what niacinamide is to get a routine that uses it well.

Built for you, not a skin type

"Oily skin" isn't a routine. Your concern, your skin, your experience level — all three shape what you get.

No pressure, no shame

Breakouts, dullness, sensitivity — normal skin things, talked about like normal skin things.

Lumira is a fictional brand, created as a portfolio demonstration project — not a real skincare company.
